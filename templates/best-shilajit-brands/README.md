Frequently Asked Questions
Which Shilajit brand is number one in Pakistan?
Chitral House is ranked number one for 2026-27 because of their PCSIR and Eurofins lab testing, batch transparency, and strong customer trust.

Is Chitral Herbs better than Chitral House?

How much does original Shilajit cost in Pakistan?
Original Shilajit resin usually costs between PKR 1,500 and PKR 3,500 for 10 grams. Chitral House is PKR 2,500, Vitalis Living is PKR 1,800, and Chitral Herbs is PKR 2,900.

Which Shilajit is best for testosterone?
Chitral House and Vigour Shilajit are most often recommended by Pakistani nutritionists for testosterone support because of their high fulvic acid content.

Can I buy Shilajit online in Pakistan?
Yes. Safe places to buy include Daraz Mall (look for the Mall badge), the official websites of Chitral House and Chitral Herbs, and the Vitalis Living official store. Avoid Facebook Marketplace and random WhatsApp sellers.

How can I test if my Shilajit is real?
Drop a pea-sized piece into warm water. Real Shilajit will dissolve completely within 2 to 3 minutes and leave no residue. If you see grit or sand at the bottom, it is fake.

What to Look for When Buying Shilajit
Not all Shilajit is the same. Here are a few things to check before you buy.

Lab testing is the most important thing. A good brand will publish PCSIR or Eurofins reports showing fulvic acid levels and heavy metal testing. If a brand cannot show you a lab report, do not buy it.

Price matters. Real Shilajit is rare and expensive to collect. If you see 10 grams for less than PKR 1,500, it is almost certainly fake or heavily diluted.

Check for DRAP registration. Since 2024, packaged Shilajit in Pakistan must display a DRAP registration number, batch number, and expiry date. Avoid products that do not have these.

Resin is better than capsules for absorption. Capsules are convenient, but resin is absorbed faster by the body. If you can handle the bitter taste, go for resin.

# well transitioned™ | Employee Exit Management & Outplacement Services

Coaching Season provides immediate, human-centered outplacement support through well transitioned™ — a 6-week 1-on-1 career coaching program that activates within the first hour of an employee separation. Built for HR leaders who believe exits should be handled with clarity, dignity, and care.

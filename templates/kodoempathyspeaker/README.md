# KODO Empathetic Audio Template

A full brand and web design system for a premium audio brand engineered for people with hearing disabilities. Not a medical device a lifestyle product. Speakers, headphones, and soundbars built around haptic resonance, bone conduction, and adaptive EQ, so music is something you feel, not just hear.
Identity built on Obsidian and Ember. Logo mark, color system, typography, motion tokens, and a 91-variable Figma design system all designed around one belief that people who hear differently deserve the same quality of experience as everyone else.
No pity. No clinical language. Just premium audio, engineered with empathy. 🖤

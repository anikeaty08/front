# Legau - drafting and review for solo practicioners

In solo and small law practices, legal work too often gets squeezed into the leftover hours. Attorneys are expected to be lawyers, administrators, marketers, and business managers all at once. Legau helps them get more legal work done when the day is already pulling them in every direction. Embedded directly in Word and Outlook, Legau brings the right legal context (your standards, templates, document management content, matter context, case law, and more) into drafting and review, turning it into practical support across legal work, not just contracts.
